Cyber Exercise

MDO Spring '26 · 4-man red team

Red Team 30+ cadets MiTM · DDoS · Zero-days SQLi · XSS

Built with classmates, this large-scale cyber exercise trained 30+ cadets in realistic, multi-domain mission environments. We simulated complex real-world operations by integrating multi-component communication across air and ground units.

Red team
  • Operated in a 4-man red team executing advanced offensive tactics — MiTM, DDoS, and zero-days.
  • Implemented realistic vulnerabilities — SQL injection and XSS — to train participants in defense and mitigation.
